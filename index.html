    A pet is not a record — it is your alleles drawn. And an allele is derived from a rappid’s mint-once tail, never stored and never assigned. So the pet cannot be re-rolled, faked, or edited: same tail, same pet, on every machine, forever. Everything below is computed on your device.

    Verify this yourself

    This page implements the reference exactly. Run it against any tail:

    import hashlib
def allele(trait, tail, bits=16):
    h = hashlib.sha256(f"rapp/1:allele:{trait}\n{tail}".encode()).hexdigest()
    return int(h[:bits // 4], 16)

    Only glow is tiered, because the spec’s bands are defined over 16 bits. Bands are exclusive, so the odds are the band widths: common < 0xC000 (3 in 4) · uncommon 0xC000–0xEFFF (~1 in 5) · rare 0xF000–0xFEFF (~1 in 17) · ultra 0xFF00–0xFFFE (~1 in 257) · mythic 0xFFFF (1 in 65,536). coat, tempo and voice are 8-bit variety — they colour the pet, they do not rank it.

    Not a token: an allele is a property of an identity, and identity is mint-once, so there is nothing to transfer. Not a gate: a mythic coat buys you a mythic coat and nothing else. Fusion (the Neon mechanic) is specified but not implemented — it needs §10 signatures and estate-owner authority, and claiming it works before signing exists would be a false-authority defect.
